Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Demo 1 is run by Benjamin James Crickmore ("we"). It helps creators and their teams upload finished short videos to their own accounts on YouTube and other platforms on a schedule, and see how those videos perform. This policy explains what information we access, collect, store, use and share, and the choices you have.

YouTube API Services

Demo 1 uses YouTube API Services. When you connect a YouTube channel, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Demo 1's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we access and collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), or the Google or Microsoft account you sign in with, and the workspaces you belong to.
  • A YouTube channel you connect, with its owner's permission given on Google's own sign-in screen: the channel's ID, name, handle, picture and subscriber and view counts; its uploaded videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, watch time); its YouTube Analytics reports; and the comments on its videos. Estimated revenue is read only if you switch on earnings for that channel. We never see or store your Google or YouTube password.
  • Accounts on other platforms you connect (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat): the account's name, handle, picture, follower count and the statistics of posts made through Demo 1.
  • Videos you add, with their titles, hashtags, settings and schedule.
  • Sign-in permissions (tokens) that let Demo 1 act for a connected account. They are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) and used only for the things described here.
  • Security records: when and from which browser you sign in, and an activity log of changes made in your workspace.

How we use it

  • To upload the videos you queue to the accounts you choose, at the times you schedule, with the visibility you choose.
  • To show each account's statistics, analytics and comments to the people in its workspace, and to post the replies and comments they approve.
  • To suggest titles, hashtags and comment replies, which your team can edit or turn off.
  • To keep accounts working and safe: checking that each connection still works, pausing an account when something looks wrong, and telling the right people.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use YouTube data to learn about channels other than your own.

Who we share it with

  • People in your workspace: the members and clients you invite see the accounts and data you give them access to.
  • Anthropic, our AI provider, receives a video's title, hashtags, spoken words and the comments being answered, only to write the suggestions described above. Under Anthropic's commercial terms this data is not used to train its models.
  • The platforms themselves (YouTube and any other platform you connect) receive the videos, titles, comments and replies you post to them.
  • Service providers that host and run Demo 1 (servers, storage and email), only to provide the service.
  • When the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • YouTube data is refreshed at least every 30 days while a channel stays connected, or deleted.
  • When you disconnect a channel in Demo 1, we revoke its sign-in permission straight away and delete its YouTube data within 7 days. If its permission is revoked elsewhere or stops working, its YouTube data is deleted within 30 days.
  • Video files are deleted a set time after they post (30 days unless your workspace chooses otherwise).
  • Everything else is kept while your account exists, and deleted within 7 days of a deletion request.

Cookies and your device

Demo 1 stores a few cookies in your browser: one that keeps you signed in, one that recognises a device you have signed in on before, and ones that remember your language, currency, look and menu layout. We use no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. This website itself uses no cookies.

Your choices

  • Disconnect an account at any time: open the account in Demo 1, then Settings, then Disconnect.
  • Revoke Demo 1's access to a YouTube channel at any time on the Google security settings page.
  • Ask us to delete your data: in Settings, Workspace, choose Request data deletion, or write to bencrickmore99@gmail.com. We delete it within 7 days. Deleting data in Demo 1 doesn't delete anything already posted on YouTube or other platforms.

Children

Demo 1 is a tool for creators and businesses and is not meant for anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change how we use your information beyond what this policy covers, we will ask you to accept the new policy before going on.

Contact

Questions or complaints about your information: bencrickmore99@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service.
